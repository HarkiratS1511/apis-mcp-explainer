14 Two kinds of job

Look things up, or take action.

Some jobs only look. Others change things. You choose which the AI may do.

Look things up

Read

Looking only. Nothing is changed.

  • Find a documentSearches your files and shows the result.
  • Check your calendarLooks at your events. Changes nothing.
  • Pull official statisticsFetches official figures and the source.

Take action

Write

Locked

  • Book a meetingAdds an event to your calendar.
  • Draft an email for you to checkWrites it. You read it and press send.
  • Make a designCreates a design from your request.

Only if you allow it, one step at a time.
