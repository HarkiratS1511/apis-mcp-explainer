18 An open standard

MCP no longer belongs to one company.

A shared industry standard, like USB. That’s why most AI tools now support it.

  1. Anthropic, the company behind Claude, releases MCP as an open standard.

  2. OpenAI and Google add support in their own AI tools.

  3. Anthropic hands MCP to the Agentic AI Foundation, part of the Linux Foundation, co-founded by Anthropic, OpenAI and Block.
